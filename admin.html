Setup Guide

Step-by-step instructions to go live

01

Add Your Business

Go to the Businesses tab and click "Add Business". Fill in your business name, category, and Google Review URL.

💡 The Google Review URL is the most important field. Everything else is optional.
02

Get Your Google Review URL

  1. Go to business.google.com
  2. Sign in and click your business
  3. Click "Get more reviews" or "Ask for reviews"
  4. Copy the URL (looks like https://g.page/r/XXXXX/review)
⚠️ If you don't see "Get more reviews", your business may not be verified yet.
03

Download & Print QR Code

  1. Go to the QR Code tab
  2. Click Download QR (PNG)
  3. Print at minimum 5×5 cm size
  4. Scan with your phone to test before printing in bulk
04

Add WhatsApp for Alerts

In your business settings, add your WhatsApp number (with country code, e.g. +919876543210).

When a customer gives 1-3 stars, you'll instantly receive a private message with their complaint — so you can resolve it before they leave.

05

Deploy Online (Recommended)

For copy-to-clipboard to work on phones, the app must be on HTTPS:

  1. Go to netlify.com/drop
  2. Drag this entire folder onto the page
  3. Get your free HTTPS URL instantly
  4. Update your QR code with the new URL
06

Optional: AI Reviews

The system includes a built-in AI that works without any configuration. For even better reviews, add a free Gemini API key:

  1. Go to aistudio.google.com
  2. Click "Create API key"
  3. Paste the key into your business settings
